# Lesson 01 — Professional Introduction

**Phase:** 1 · Foundation & Professional Communication  
**Task:** 用 60–90 秒向日本企业的 HR、招聘经理或国际团队介绍自己。  
**Target:** 先做到清晰、可信、有重点；再逐步提升自然度。

## 1. Learning outcomes

完成本课后，你应该能够：
- 说明自己的职业身份和经验范围；
- 概括当前专业方向；
- 用一个项目证明自己的能力；
- 说明自己希望承担的职责；
- 在追问下补充细节。

## 2. A reliable structure

使用五段结构：

1. **Who I am** — 职业身份
2. **What I do** — 核心职责与能力
3. **What I have delivered** — 代表成果
4. **What I focus on now** — 当前关注方向
5. **What I am looking for** — 下一步职业方向

不要一开始就罗列所有技术名词。先让听众理解你解决什么问题、承担什么责任。

## 3. Model introduction

> Hello, I'm Qi. I'm a technology and product leader with over ten years of experience in enterprise software development.
>
> Over the past few years, I've focused on applying AI to real business workflows, particularly in the financial services industry.
>
> My experience covers the full lifecycle, from understanding business requirements and designing solutions to leading development teams and delivering enterprise-grade systems.
>
> For example, I led the development of an AI-powered post-loan risk management platform for a commercial bank. I worked across product design, architecture, engineering and stakeholder coordination.
>
> My strength is bridging business needs and AI engineering. I'm particularly interested in roles where I can lead both business-oriented AI initiatives and technical execution.

### 中文理解

你好，我叫 Qi。我是一名技术与产品负责人，在企业软件开发领域有十年以上经验。

过去几年，我主要专注于将 AI 应用于真实业务流程，尤其是金融服务行业。

我的经验覆盖完整交付周期，从理解业务需求、设计解决方案，到带领开发团队和交付企业级系统。

例如，我曾主导一家商业银行的贷后风险管理智能平台开发，负责产品设计、架构、工程实现和多方协同。

我的优势是连接业务需求与 AI 工程。我尤其希望承担能够同时领导 AI 业务项目和技术落地的岗位。

> 注意：这是一版训练示例。正式面试时，项目细节和成果数字必须与你能够核实的实际经历一致。

## 4. Key vocabulary

| Expression | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| technology leader | 技术负责人 | I work as a technology leader. |
| product leader | 产品负责人 | I also take on product leadership responsibilities. |
| enterprise software | 企业级软件 | I have built enterprise software for financial institutions. |
| business workflow | 业务流程 | We applied AI to a complex business workflow. |
| full lifecycle | 完整生命周期 | I have experience across the full product lifecycle. |
| stakeholder coordination | 利益相关方协调 | The project required close stakeholder coordination. |
| technical execution | 技术落地执行 | I was responsible for technical execution. |
| measurable impact | 可衡量的影响 | We focused on measurable business impact. |
| bridge A and B | 连接 A 与 B | I bridge business needs and engineering. |
| lead the development of | 主导开发 | I led the development of the platform. |

### 重点区别

- **experience in**：某领域的经验。例：experience in enterprise software.
- **experience with**：使用某技术或处理某类事物的经验。例：experience with LLM-based applications.
- **responsible for**：负责某项工作。
- **in charge of**：负责、主管，口语中常用。
- **lead**：领导、主导，强调带头推动。
- **manage**：管理，强调组织、协调和控制。

## 5. Sentence patterns

### Pattern A — 职业身份
"I'm a [role] with [number] years of experience in [field]."

例：I'm a software engineer with over ten years of experience in enterprise systems.

### Pattern B — 专业重点
"I specialize in [area], particularly [specific domain]."

例：I specialize in AI application development, particularly enterprise AI agents.

### Pattern C — 端到端职责
"My experience covers everything from [A] to [B]."

例：My experience covers everything from business analysis to solution delivery.

### Pattern D — 核心优势
"My strength is bridging [A] and [B]."

例：My strength is bridging business strategy and AI engineering.

### Pattern E — 职业目标
"I'm looking for an opportunity to [contribution]."

例：I'm looking for an opportunity to lead AI transformation initiatives.

## 6. Grammar focus — Present perfect vs. simple past

介绍职业经历时，常用现在完成时描述持续至今的经验：

- I've worked in enterprise software for over ten years.
- I've focused on AI applications in recent years.

描述已完成的具体项目时，通常用一般过去时：

- I led the development of a risk management platform.
- We delivered the first version in one month.

不要说：I am work in AI for two years.

可以说：
- I've been working on AI applications for the past two years.
- I've worked on AI applications for about two years.

## 7. Pronunciation focus

本课重点练习：
- **experience** /ɪkˈspɪriəns/：重音在第二音节附近；
- **enterprise** /ˈentərpraɪz/：注意开头重音；
- **development** /dɪˈveləpmənt/：注意重音和词尾；
- **particularly** /pərˈtɪkjələrli/：分段练习，不要逐字母读。

如果当前没有语音输入，本课只进行文本表达训练，不对实际发音打分。

## 8. Guided practice

先完成以下句子，不要直接复制示范答案：

1. I'm a ______ with over ______ years of experience in ______.
2. In recent years, I've focused on ______.
3. My experience covers ______ and ______.
4. One project I'm particularly proud of is ______.
5. My strength is ______.
6. I'm looking for an opportunity to ______.

## 9. Main speaking task

请用英文写出或说出一段 60–90 秒的自我介绍。

要求：
- 先独立完成，不必追求完美；
- 尽量使用本课 5 个以上表达；
- 讲清楚职业身份、核心能力、一个真实项目和未来方向；
- 不要逐字翻译中文简历。

## 10. Follow-up interview questions

完成自我介绍后，导师从以下问题中选择追问：

1. What was your specific role in that project?
2. What was the biggest challenge, and how did you address it?
3. How do you balance business requirements and technical constraints?
4. What kind of AI leadership role are you looking for?
5. How do you measure the business impact of an AI solution?

## 11. Self-check

提交答案后检查：
- [ ] 是否用完整句表达职业身份？
- [ ] 是否区分持续经验和已完成项目？
- [ ] 是否给出具体项目，而不是只列技术栈？
- [ ] 是否说明自己的业务价值？
- [ ] 是否能回答至少一个追问？

## 12. Evidence to capture

本课完成后记录：
- 初始自我介绍原文；
- 语法、词汇、组织、流畅、发音（有音频时）、商务得体性评分；
- 最重要的 3 个错误模式；
- 修订后的自我介绍；
- 下一次复测任务。

**Completion criterion:** 能在不看稿的情况下，用清晰的结构完成 60–90 秒介绍，并回答至少两个相关追问。一次完成不代表永久掌握，需要在后续课程复测。
